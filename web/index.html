          Push notifications with ntfy
          
    
          ntfy is a free push service: the box posts a short message to a named topic, and every phone or tablet subscribed to that topic shows a notification. There is no account to make and no key to copy — the topic name is the only thing that links the box to your family's devices.
          
    
            
      
          1. Install the app. ntfy on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, or ntfy on Google Play or F-Droid for Android. A browser at ntfy.sh works too.

          2. Pick a topic below and save it.
            3. 
      
          3. Subscribe the app to that exact topic, then send the test notification.
            4. 
    
          

    
          
      
      
          On the public server the topic name is the password. Anyone who guesses it reads every notification about the medication. Use the generated name, not grandma.
